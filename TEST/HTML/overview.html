What does “Risk” mean?
Risk is an estimate of how likely the child is to experience emotional overload soon, based on recent intensity, how often high-intensity logs occur, and the trend direction.
Low: emotions are stable or improving, and high-intensity events are rare.
Medium: emotions show some escalation or repeated stress episodes.
High: strong upward trend or frequent high-intensity events → therapist attention recommended.
